A prepared demonstration

Four independent tasks share one clock. The shuttle follows a captured road route to the airport. The van collects equipment in Pasila, waits eight minutes, then follows a second road leg to Vuosaari.

A drone inspects an illustrative harbour loop and returns. A vessel follows authored marine waypoints to a support point. These tasks demonstrate different asset models; they do not imply cargo transfers or flight/navigation clearance.

  1. Select an asset to inspect its task and assumptions.
  2. Play, pause or scrub the shared clock.
  3. Use selected-asset focus to isolate a route.
  4. Compare duration, energy and reserve at arrival.

Destinations and routes are deliberately fixed. This edition shows a curated result; it does not offer arbitrary trip planning.
